## About Me

I'm an Electrical Engineer interested in **embedded systems, electronics, and system-level hardware development**.

I enjoy building hardware and figuring out why it doesn't work. My projects have taken me from **custom PCB design and bring-up** to **embedded firmware, FPGA/VHDL, digital signal processing, mixed-signal systems, and autonomous robotics**.

I'm especially interested in the point where those areas come together, integrating hardware, firmware, sensors, communications, and control into a complete physical system.

I'm continuing to build my experience in **embedded hardware, RF, FPGA/DSP, hardware testing, and system integration**, with a particular interest in robotics, aerospace, and other complex electromechanical systems.
